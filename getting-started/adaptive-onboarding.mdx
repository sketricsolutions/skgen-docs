Adaptive Onboarding is a project-based conversation with the SketricGen onboarding strategist. It learns enough about your business and experience to recommend a useful first agent instead of showing the same setup checklist to everyone.

You can provide a public website URL for faster personalization, or describe what you sell, who you serve, and where you operate. Sharing a website is optional.

![Selecting a role and AI-agent experience before the onboarding strategist asks for business context](/images/product-ui-2026/adaptive-onboarding-preferences.gif)

The walkthrough above stops before any website research begins. The strategist first adjusts the onboarding depth to the user's role and AI-agent experience.

## What Adaptive Onboarding Does

Adaptive Onboarding can:

- Use your role and AI-agent experience to adjust its explanations.
- Review public information from a website you provide.
- Identify likely business, website, marketing, or automation bottlenecks.
- Research market or growth signals when that evidence can change the recommendation.
- Recommend live SketricGen templates that match your goal.
- Offer to start Brand Agent setup while the onboarding conversation continues.
- Save the conversation and recommendations to the selected Project so you can return later.

It does not need to perform a marketing audit for every user. If your need is operational, support-related, data-focused, or unrelated to growth, the strategist can recommend a more relevant workflow instead.

## Complete Adaptive Onboarding

1. Select the role that best matches your work.
2. Select how comfortable you are with AI agents.
3. Start the onboarding conversation.
4. Provide your website URL, or describe your business without a URL.
5. Explain the first problem you want an agent to solve.
6. Review the strategist's observations and recommendations.
7. Choose whether to start a Brand Agent, open a recommended template, or continue to Agent Space.

The right side of the onboarding screen shows completed and in-progress activity. The status at the top changes while the strategist is researching or waiting for you.

## Website and Growth Research

When a website is available, the onboarding strategist may inspect:

- Public products, services, positioning, audience, and brand language
- Website structure and visible conversion paths
- Public performance or technical signals when available
- Public search, competitor, and market evidence when relevant
- Live SketricGen templates that match the identified need

The resulting report should distinguish observed facts from third-party estimates and recommendations. Search-volume, traffic, advertising, and competitor data are estimates unless they come from an analytics account you connect separately.

<Note>
  Adaptive Onboarding does not automatically gain access to private analytics, CRM data, advertising accounts, or internal documents. Website onboarding starts with the public information you provide.
</Note>

## Brand Agent Setup in Parallel

If a website is available, the strategist can offer to start a website-trained Brand Agent. A Brand Agent can learn the website and brand, answer visitor questions, guide visitors to useful pages, and capture leads.

Brand Agent setup does not start without your choice. When you select **Create Brand Agent**, setup opens separately and the onboarding conversation stays available. You can continue discussing your goals while the Brand Agent is prepared.

See [Create a Brand Agent](/brand-agents/create-brand-agent) for the complete setup and review process.

## Project Storage and Control

The onboarding conversation is scoped to the selected Teamspace and Project. This means:

- Switching Projects changes which onboarding state is loaded.
- You can leave and later return to the same Project's onboarding conversation.
- Skipping onboarding does not delete the saved conversation.
- Recommended templates and Brand Agent actions remain choices; they are not executed automatically.

## Troubleshooting

### "We couldn’t prepare your onboarding"

SketricGen could not load your Teamspace, Project, or saved onboarding state. Select **Try again**. If the problem continues, select **Skip Onboarding** and open Agent Space, then contact support.

### "Project storage is temporarily unavailable."

The strategist may have finished, but the latest conversation state could not be saved. Keep the page open and retry when the storage message clears. Do not restart the onboarding in another Project.

### The website cannot be analyzed

Check that the URL is public and accessible without a login. You can continue without it by describing what you sell, your audience, and your main geography.

## Capability Facts

- **Product surface:** Adaptive Onboarding
- **Primary input:** role, AI-agent experience, optional website, and business goal
- **Outputs:** visible recommendations, optional research report, template suggestions, and optional Brand Agent setup
- **Can use:** public website analysis, public research, market signals, and the live template catalog
- **Cannot access by default:** private analytics, internal files, advertising accounts, or CRM data
- **User control:** the user chooses whether to start a Brand Agent, use a template, skip onboarding, or open Agent Space
- **Storage scope:** selected Teamspace, Project, and user
- **Next step:** [Open Agent Space](/ai-workforce/agent-space) or [create a Brand Agent](/brand-agents/create-brand-agent)
