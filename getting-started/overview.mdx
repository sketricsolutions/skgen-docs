SketricGen is a no-code platform for building AI agents that work with your business context, tools, files, and teammates. You can create customer-facing Brand Agents or an internal AI Workforce.

The platform separates **where agents work** from **how agents are configured**:

- [Agent Space](/ai-workforce/agent-space) is the live workplace where you see agents, active work, schedules, teammates, and requests needing attention.
- [Agent Build](/ai-workforce/agent-build) is the internal configuration surface for instructions, models, sub-agents, tools, skills, and handoffs.
- [Playground](/ai-workforce/playground) is the Run experience for testing, conversations, files, artifacts, traces, and human input.

## Start With Your Business Context

[Adaptive Onboarding](/getting-started/adaptive-onboarding) can learn your role, AI experience, public website, and current bottleneck. It then recommends a practical first agent or live template.

If you provide a website, the onboarding strategist can analyze public business and brand information. When relevant, it can research growth signals and offer to start a Brand Agent while the onboarding conversation continues.

You remain in control of which recommendations to use. SketricGen does not automatically connect private accounts, publish content, or start a Brand Agent without your action.

## Brand Agents

[Brand Agents](/brand-agents/overview) are customer-facing agents trained on your website, Brand DNA, Knowledge Base, and approved tools.

Use a Brand Agent to:

- Answer visitor questions from your information
- Guide visitors to relevant pages
- Capture leads inside a conversation
- Support product discovery and customer intake
- Deploy a branded widget or fullscreen experience

## AI Workforce

[AI Workforce](/ai-workforce/overview) is for internal work across research, marketing, content, sales, support, data, and operations.

AI Workforce agents can:

- Use built-in tools and connected business apps
- Delegate work to specialist sub-agents
- Use reusable skills and Knowledge Bases
- Work with files and create artifacts
- Pause for human input or approval
- Run manually or on a schedule
- Share conversations and operational state with permitted teammates

## How the Main Surfaces Connect

1. Adaptive Onboarding or a template helps you choose a useful starting point.
2. Agent Space shows the agents available in the selected Project.
3. Agent Build defines how an agent works internally.
4. Playground runs the draft or published agent.
5. Traces, files, and human-input requests show what happened during the run.
6. Runs and schedules repeat useful work after it has been tested.
