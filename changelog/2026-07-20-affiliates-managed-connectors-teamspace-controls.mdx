This release expands the ways you can grow with SketricGen and gives workspace owners clearer control over plans and resources. It introduces affiliate commissions, makes SketricGen-managed connectors broadly available, strengthens premium model and Trial plan handling, and raises knowledge base allowances.

## TL;DR

- **Affiliate program:** Refer new customers, track eligible activity, and earn commissions.
- **Managed connectors:** Use supported apps with SketricGen-managed access and transparent credit billing.
- **Clear premium model access:** Premium models require an active paid subscription, with a direct Trial plan checkout path when appropriate.
- **More Trial plan control:** Trial checkout and cancellation now use dedicated confirmation dialogs.
- **Delete unused free Teamspaces:** Owners can remove eligible free Teamspaces and their associated project records.
- **Higher knowledge base allowances:** Plan tiers now support up to 25 knowledge bases, with updated size limits for each knowledge base.
- **More reliable email:** Verification and Teamspace invitation emails use improved production delivery.
- **Fresher blog pages:** The blog index is less likely to show stale content or fail while new posts are published.

---

## What's new

### 1) Earn with the SketricGen affiliate program

SketricGen now includes a fuller affiliate experience for partners and advocates:

- **Referral codes:** Share your referral identity with prospective customers.
- **Eligible signup tracking:** Attribute new accounts that join through your referral.
- **Subscription benefits:** Track qualifying subscription activity connected to referred customers.
- **Commission tracking:** Eligible affiliate commissions are recorded as subscription events are processed.

**Why it matters:** Creators, consultants, and partners can recommend SketricGen through a structured program and receive credit for customers they introduce.

---

### 2) SketricGen-managed connectors and credit billing

Supported connectors can now use credentials managed by SketricGen:

- **Managed app access:** Connect supported apps without providing your own provider credential where managed access is offered.
- **Credit-based usage:** Eligible managed connector actions consume SketricGen credits.
- **Current app catalog:** The Apps experience uses the latest managed connector availability.
- **Project-aware access:** Connector identity continues to follow the Teamspace project that uses it.

**Why it matters:** More teams can connect useful business apps without separately sourcing and maintaining every underlying integration credential.

---

### 3) Premium models and Trial plan controls

Model access and trial decisions now have clearer account checks:

- **Paid model access:** Premium models require an active paid subscription.
- **Plan-aware guidance:** Locked models indicate that a subscription is required.
- **Trial checkout dialog:** Eligible users receive a focused path for starting a Trial plan.
- **Trial cancellation confirmation:** Cancelling a trial requires a clear confirmation step.

**Why it matters:** You know when a model requires a paid plan, and important trial decisions are less likely to happen accidentally.

---

### 4) Stronger Teamspace ownership controls

Owners have more control over unused free workspaces:

- **Owner-only deletion:** Only the Teamspace owner can delete an eligible free Teamspace or project.
- **Associated cleanup:** Deletion removes the related workspace records rather than leaving an incomplete project behind.
- **Free-tier allowances:** Newly created free Teamspaces receive the appropriate starting limits.
- **Active billing protection:** Paid Teamspaces follow their subscription management flow instead of the free-workspace deletion path.

**Why it matters:** Owners can clean up unused free workspaces while destructive account actions remain restricted to the right person.

---

### 5) Higher knowledge base capacity

Knowledge base allowances have been expanded across plan tiers:

- **Up to 25 knowledge bases:** Plan tiers now support a higher total number of knowledge bases.
- **Updated per-knowledge-base limits:** Individual size allowances have been adjusted with the new plan structure.
- **Teamspace-wide enforcement:** Usage is evaluated for the active Teamspace so members see consistent capacity.

**Why it matters:** You can organize more sources by product, department, client, or use case without combining everything into a small number of knowledge bases.

---

### 6) More dependable emails and blog updates

Several reliability improvements make important communication and public content more dependable:

- **Account emails:** Production verification messages use improved delivery infrastructure.
- **Teamspace invitations:** Invite emails use the same more reliable sending path.
- **Fresher blog index:** Newly published posts and updates are less likely to be hidden by stale page data.
- **Safer service credentials:** Internal credential handling was strengthened without changing how you connect supported apps in the interface.

**Why it matters:** Important account messages are more likely to arrive, and public content remains available as it changes.

## How to use (quick steps)

### Use the affiliate program

1. Open the available affiliate area in your SketricGen account.
2. Copy your referral code or referral link.
3. Share it with an appropriate audience.
4. Return to the affiliate area to review eligible referrals and commissions.

### Connect a managed app

1. Open **Tools** or the **Apps** catalog in the correct Teamspace project.
2. Select an app that offers SketricGen-managed access.
3. Complete the connection flow and review its available actions.
4. Test the agent and monitor credit usage for managed connector actions.

### Delete an unused free Teamspace

1. Switch to the free Teamspace you own.
2. Open its settings and choose the deletion option.
3. Review the warning and confirm only after verifying that the Teamspace is no longer needed.

### Review model access

1. Open an agent's model picker.
2. Check whether the preferred model is available or marked as premium.
3. Select an available model, or follow the plan guidance to review subscription options.

## FAQ

**Who can join the affiliate program?**
Availability and eligibility are shown in the SketricGen affiliate experience. Review the current program terms before sharing a referral link.

**Do managed connectors use my own API key?**
Not when SketricGen-managed access is available and selected. Those actions use managed credentials and consume credits according to the current in-product pricing.

**Can Trial users use premium models?**
Premium models require an active paid subscription. The model picker and checkout dialogs show the options available to your account.

**Can any Teamspace member delete the workspace?**
No. Eligible free Teamspace deletion is restricted to the owner.

**How many knowledge bases can I create?**
Plan tiers now support up to 25 knowledge bases. Exact size and usage allowances are shown in the product because they can vary by plan.

**Will deleting a free Teamspace affect a paid subscription?**
The free-Teamspace deletion flow is separate from paid subscription management. Always confirm the selected Teamspace and review the warning before deleting it.
